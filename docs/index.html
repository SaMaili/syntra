About Syntra

Syntra focuses on usability, accessibility, and a visually appealing interface to help you build better social habits. The app leverages modern Flutter technology to provide a rich, native experience across all devices.

With features like persistent storage, local notifications, and comprehensive progress tracking, Syntra makes personal growth engaging and measurable.

  • ✓ Completely private - all data stored locally on your device
  • ✓ No ads or tracking - focus on your growth
  • ✓ Offline functionality - works without internet
  • ✓ Regular updates and new challenges
