Privacy Policy for Syntra

Effective Date: January 8, 2025

Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Syntra ("we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile application (the "App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about our data practices.

Information We Collect

Information Automatically Collected

  • Device Information: We may collect information about your device, including device model, operating system version, and app version for crash reporting and performance optimization.
  • Usage Data: We collect data about how you interact with the App, including challenge completion statistics, settings preferences, and app usage patterns. This data is stored locally on your device.

Information You Provide

  • Challenge Responses: Any responses or interactions you make with challenges are stored locally on your device.
  • Settings and Preferences: Your app settings, including notification preferences, language selection, and theme choices.

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain the App's functionality
  • To deliver notifications and reminders as requested by you
  • To track your progress and statistics within the App
  • To personalize your experience based on your preferences
  • To improve the App's performance and features

Data Storage and Security

Local Storage

  • All personal data and challenge progress are stored locally on your device using secure local databases (SQLite)
  • Your data does not leave your device unless you explicitly choose to share it
  • We do not transmit personal information to external servers

Security Measures

  • We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information
  • Data is encrypted using industry-standard encryption methods
  • Access to your data is restricted to the App's functionality only

Permissions We Request

The App requests the following permissions:

  • Notifications: To send you challenge reminders and motivational messages
  • Vibration: To provide haptic feedback during interactions
  • System Alert Window: To display notifications even when the App is not active
  • Wake Lock: To ensure notifications are delivered on time
  • Boot Completed: To restore notifications after device restart
  • Exact Alarms: To schedule precise notification timing

Third-Party Services

Flutter Framework

The App is built using Google's Flutter framework, which may collect anonymous usage statistics to improve the framework. This data collection is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Audio Assets

The App includes audio files that are stored locally and do not require internet connection or external data transmission.

Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

Data Retention

  • Your data is retained locally on your device for as long as the App is installed
  • You can delete all data by uninstalling the App
  • Challenge statistics and preferences are reset when you clear the App's data through your device settings

Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to:

  • Access: View all data stored by the App through the statistics and settings screens
  • Delete: Remove all data by uninstalling the App or clearing App data
  • Control Notifications: Disable notifications through the App settings or device settings
  • Modify Settings: Change language, theme, and other preferences at any time

Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, or rent your personal information to third parties. All data remains on your device and under your control.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted within the App and will take effect immediately upon posting. Your continued use of the App after any changes indicates your acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.

International Data Transfers

Since all data is stored locally on your device, no international data transfers occur.

Additional Information

Open Source Components

The App may include open source software components. These components are governed by their respective licenses and privacy policies.

Analytics

We do not use third-party analytics services. All usage tracking is performed locally on your device for your personal statistics only.

Advertising

The App does not display advertisements or use advertising networks.
